Privacy notice

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

This site is run by TRTek LLC. Email trtekllc@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

What we collect

  • Job checker: the job link you paste. We use a scrambled version of your network address to limit abuse and delete it within about an hour.
  • Weekly report: your email address.
  • Candidate pool: your name, email, current title, years of experience, target roles, location, optional LinkedIn link, and your resume, plus anything else your resume contains.
  • Recruiter pilot: your name, work email, company, title, the roles you're hiring for, and your answer about reply commitments.
  • Campaign source: if you arrive from a marketing link, we record which campaign sent you.

Please leave sensitive details out of your resume, such as your Social Security number, date of birth, or health information.

How we use it

  • To review your application and contact you about the pilot.
  • To send the weekly report if you asked for it.
  • To run, protect and improve this service.

During the pilot, a person reviews candidate applications. We don't use automated tools to rank candidates or decide who is accepted. We'll update this notice before that changes.

Who can see it

  • Employers and recruiters: we won't share your resume or personal details with an employer unless you say yes to that specific employer first.
  • Service providers: Supabase stores our data, Cloudflare hosts this site, and Google Fonts supplies the page fonts. Google may receive your network address when the fonts load. We'll add our email provider here when we start sending email.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep candidate and recruiter information for up to 12 months after your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We remove your report email when you ask us to.

Your choices

Email trtekllc@gmail.com from the address you used to see, correct or delete your information, or to leave the pool. Depending on where you live, you may have other privacy rights, and we'll respond to those requests too.

Who this is for

This service is for adults in the United States. Please don't submit information if you're under 18.

Security

Resumes are kept in private storage that isn't available through a public link. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this notice changes, and we'll let you know about significant changes that affect your application.
